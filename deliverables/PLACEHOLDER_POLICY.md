# Placeholder Policy

Canonical placeholder syntax: `{{UPPER_SNAKE_CASE}}`.

## Must be placeholders
- project/user-specific names;
- dates and periods;
- tasks/actions;
- progress and KPI values;
- owners;
- evidence;
- tracks and milestones;
- content themes;
- runtime state derived from examples.

## May remain literal
- labels;
- schema enums;
- layout identifiers;
- static instructions;
- geometry;
- normative constraints.

A reusable template containing filled example data is non-compliant until parameterized.
